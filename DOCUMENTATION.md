# 📚 Documentation Guide

## Using Each Financial Tool

### 1️⃣ Business Expense Tracker
- **Purpose**: Track daily, weekly, and monthly expenses
- **Features**: Category-wise breakdown, trend analysis, reports
- **Best for**: All businesses

### 2️⃣ GST Tax Tracker
- **Purpose**: Calculate and track GST liability
- **Features**: Tax calculations, compliance reports, input/output tracking
- **Best for**: India-based businesses

### 3️⃣ Invoice Tracker
- **Purpose**: Manage client invoices and payments
- **Features**: Invoice generation, payment tracking, aging reports
- **Best for**: Service providers, consultants

### 4️⃣ Break-Even Calculator
- **Purpose**: Determine profitability threshold
- **Features**: Cost analysis, break-even point, profit projections
- **Best for**: Business planning

### 5️⃣ Budget vs Actual
- **Purpose**: Compare planned vs actual spending
- **Features**: Variance analysis, trend charts, forecasting
- **Best for**: Finance managers

### 6️⃣ Monthly Financial Report
- **Purpose**: Executive-level financial overview
- **Features**: P&L statement, balance sheet, key metrics
- **Best for**: Business owners, stakeholders

### 7️⃣ Payroll Calculator
- **Purpose**: Manage employee salaries and deductions
- **Features**: Tax calculation, benefits, compliance
- **Best for**: HR and finance teams

### 8️⃣ Cash Flow Control
- **Purpose**: Monitor and forecast cash movements
- **Features**: Cash inflow/outflow, forecasts, liquidity analysis
- **Best for**: Finance planning

## Common Tips

✅ **Data Entry**: Always validate data before entering
✅ **Formulas**: Don't modify locked formulas unless experienced
✅ **Backups**: Keep monthly backups of your data
✅ **Updates**: Update data regularly for accurate reports
✅ **Sharing**: Use Excel's share feature for team collaboration

---

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Formula errors?**
A: Check data types - ensure numbers are formatted correctly

**Q: Charts not updating?**
A: Refresh data source and recalculate (Ctrl+Shift+F9)

**Q: File won't open?**
A: Update Excel to latest version or use Google Sheets
